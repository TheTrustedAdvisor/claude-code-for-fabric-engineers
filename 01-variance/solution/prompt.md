# Bikes at Mythenquai, September 2025

Goal: From bikes-2025-09.csv, find how many bikes the Mythenquai counter counted in September 2025 in total, and on which calendar day it counted the most (date and count).

Constraints:
- Read only bikes-2025-09.csv and counters.csv. Don't change either file.
- Find the counter through counters.csv. Bikes are both directions together; pedestrians don't count.
- Compute the numbers with a script, don't estimate.
- Write the result to results/mythenquai.md, at most ten lines: the two numbers, then one sentence on how you computed them.

Audience: the city's transport committee, traffic experts, not programmers.
