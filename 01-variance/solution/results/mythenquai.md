# Bikes at Mythenquai, September 2025

- **Total bikes, September 2025:** 61,677 (both directions, Innenstadt and Wollishofen)
- **Busiest day:** Saturday, 20 September 2025, with 3,586 bikes

A script found the Mythenquai bike counter in counters.csv (ID 5002). It then added up the bikes counted in both directions over all 2,880 quarter-hour intervals in September (30 full days, no gaps), using the raw counts without the listed correction factor of 0.98.
